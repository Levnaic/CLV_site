About us

In the heart of Frankfurt-Niederrad, fans, neighbours and friends meet at our place. Bundesliga, Champions League or a relaxed evening after work – there is always something going on.

Big screens, proper sound and a team that knows how to pour a good drink. Welcome to C'est la vie Sports Bar.

Live Sport

Never miss a match – we show the sport you want to see.

  1. Bundesliga

    Every matchday live, including the Saturday conference.

  2. Eintracht Frankfurt

    Home or away – watch the SGE from kick-off to the final whistle.

  3. Champions League

    Europe's best teams on midweek evenings.

  4. More highlights

    Formula 1, darts, internationals and major tournaments.

  5. Screens & Sound

    Several big screens and sound that lets you hear every goal.
