Frequently asked questions

Is there a sports bar near Deutsche Bank Park?

Yes. Our sports bar in Frankfurt-Niederrad is not far from Deutsche Bank Park: about 2 km, roughly a 25-minute walk.

Watch Eintracht Frankfurt with us →

Is there a bar near Festhalle Frankfurt?

Yes. Our sports bar in Frankfurt-Niederrad is about 3 km from the Festhalle in a straight line – a good stop for a drink before or after the concert.

All places nearby →

How far is the bar from Frankfurt Hauptbahnhof (main station)?

About 2.5 km in a straight line. Our sports bar is south of the main station, across the river Main in Niederrad.

All places nearby →

Where is C'est la vie Sports Bar?

C'est la vie Sports Bar in Frankfurt-Niederrad: Schwarzwaldstraße 79, 60528 Frankfurt am Main.

What kinds of beer do you have?

Fresh on tap: Pils, wheat beer and shandy. Also on the menu: alcohol-free beer. Sizes and prices are in the drinks menu.

Can I choose the music?

Yes, at our bar the guests decide what music is playing.
